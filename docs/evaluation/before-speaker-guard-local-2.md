# Defining Activation Milestones for B2B Products

As a B2B product manager, defining what it means for a user to be "activated" on your platform is crucial for determining the effectiveness of your onboarding process and long-term retention strategies.

## Step 1: Establishing a Clear Activation Metric for B2B Products

Airtable's activation team focused on a single north star metric: what they called their team activation metric. This metric was determined to be highly correlated with long-term retention and required a number of things to be true, including substance built, persistence, and collaboration on a weekly cadence [1].

### Recommendation:
Identify a specific, measurable milestone that indicates a user is engaged with your platform. This could be a combination of factors such as building substance, persisting over time, and collaborating with others on a regular basis.

## Step 2: Defining Activation Milestones for B2B Products

Ben Williams from Snyk emphasizes the importance of defining activation milestones for teams, rather than individual users. According to Ben, activation is about forming a habit around the usage of a product, specifically deriving core value by fixing vulnerabilities [2].

### Recommendation:
Define activation milestones for teams, rather than individual users. This approach recognizes that security is a team effort and that different team members may fulfill different parts of the activation journey.

## Step 3: Identifying Key Activation Metrics

Albert Cheng from Duolingo emphasizes the importance of identifying key activation metrics, such as daily frequency and retention rate. According to Albert, retention rate is a critical metric for determining the stickiness of a product [3].

### Recommendation:
Identify key activation metrics that align with your product's goals and user behavior. This may include metrics such as daily frequency, retention rate, and user engagement.

## Step 4: Optimizing Onboarding for Growth

Merci Grace emphasizes the importance of optimizing onboarding for growth, rather than just retention.

### Recommendation:
Optimize onboarding for growth, rather than just retention. Think about onboarding from the user's perspective and consider how to introduce your product in a way that is engaging and valuable.

## Step 5: Embracing a Growth Mindset

Crystal Widjaja from Gojek emphasizes the importance of embracing a growth mindset when it comes to onboarding and growth.

### Recommendation:
Embrace a growth mindset when it comes to onboarding and growth. Focus on user acquisition and retention, and be willing to experiment and iterate on your approach.
