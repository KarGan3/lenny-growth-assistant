# Activation Guidance for B2B Onboarding

## Understanding Activation Milestones

Defining the right activation milestones for your onboarding process is crucial for measuring user engagement and retention.

### Suggested Application

To define your own activation milestones, consider the following:

* Identify key user behaviors that indicate engagement and retention
* Establish a clear activation metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention
* Set a high bar for activation milestones to ensure substance and commitment from users

## Step 1: Establishing a Clear Activation Metric

* Identify key user behaviors that indicate engagement and retention
* Establish a clear activation metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention
* Examples:
	+ Airtable: A team of people are activated on the product and using it in a way that suggests long-term retention. [1]
	+ Snyk: A user fixes vulnerabilities, indicating a habit around the usage of the product. [2]
	+ Grammarly: An activation installation aha moment that carries the user for a long time. [3]
	+ Gojek: Filling gaps in the product, such as SMS provider selection, to improve onboarding and user success. [4]

## Step 2: Defining Activation Milestones

* Identify key user behaviors that indicate engagement and retention
* Establish a clear activation milestone that marks the beginning of long-term retention
* Examples:
	+ Airtable: A team of people are activated on the product and using it in a way that suggests long-term retention. [1]
	+ Snyk: A user fixes vulnerabilities, indicating a habit around the usage of the product. [2]
	+ Grammarly: An activation installation aha moment that carries the user for a long time. [3]
	+ Gojek: Filling gaps in the product, such as SMS provider selection, to improve onboarding and user success. [4]

## Step 3: Optimizing Onboarding

* Think about onboarding from the user's perspective
* Consider the introduction and framing of the product
* Examples:
	+ Designing games with onboarding in mind
	+ Focusing on the user's experience and flow
	+ Considering the steps between the user and the full value of the product

## Takeaway

Defining the right activation milestones for your onboarding process requires careful consideration of key user behaviors, clear activation metrics, and high bars for activation milestones. By following these steps and examples, you can optimize your onboarding process and improve user engagement and retention.
