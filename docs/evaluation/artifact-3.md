# Defining Activation Milestones for B2B Onboarding

As a B2B product manager, defining an effective activation milestone is crucial for driving long-term retention and business results. According to Lauryn Isford, former growth team lead at Airtable, every growth team needs a north star metric to focus on what drives business results. [1] For Airtable's activation team, the north star metric was a team activation metric, which measured the activation of a representative team using the product in a way that suggested long-term retention. [1]

## Step 1: Establishing a Clear Activation Metric for B2B Onboarding

Lauryn Isford, from Airtable, emphasizes the importance of having a singular focus on what the business results are that the growth team is working to drive. [1] In Airtable's case, the team activation metric was a key metric to determine when a user was considered "activated" [1]. This metric was based on a user's behavior on the platform, specifically the ability to collaborate with others on a weekly cadence. [1]

### Suggested application

* Identify the key behaviors or milestones that indicate a user is engaged and active on your platform. [1]

## Step 2: Setting a Clear Activation Milestone for B2B Onboarding

Ben Williams from Snyk emphasizes the importance of defining activation milestones based on team habits, rather than individual user behavior. [2] According to Ben, activation is indicative of a team forming a habit around the usage of a product, specifically deriving core value by fixing vulnerabilities. [2] This definition is crucial because security is a team sport, and teams often consist of multiple people with different roles. [2]

### Suggested application

* Identify the key habits or behaviors that indicate successful onboarding for your team-based product.

## Step 3: Focusing on Existing User Retention for B2B Onboarding

According to Cheng, companies often focus most of their energy on retaining their existing user base, as this retention rate has a compounding effect on building daily habits [3].

## Suggested Application for B2B Product Managers

Crystal Widjaja from Gojek highlights the importance of growth teams filling gaps in the product, particularly in areas like first-time user experience. [4] By embedding growth into core product teams, companies can ensure that key aspects of onboarding are addressed.

## Step 5: Designing for a Seamless Onboarding Experience

This approach involves considering the user's journey from the outset, taking into account how users will discover the product, what steps they will need to take to integrate with the product, and what value they will gain from using it. [5]

## Takeaway

To create an effective onboarding experience, it's essential to consider the user's journey from the very beginning. [5]
