# Activation Milestones for B2B Onboarding Success

## Introduction

 According to Lauryn Isford, Head of Growth at Airtable, every growth team needs a north star metric to focus on what the business results are that the team is working to drive. [1]

## Establishing a Clear Activation Metric

Airtable's activation metric was determined to be highly correlated with long-term retention. [1] This metric required a number of things to be true, including substance being built, people persisting and collaborating. [1]

## Defining Activation Milestones

Ben Williams, VP of Product at Snyk, defines activation as indicative of the team forming a habit around the usage of the product, specifically fixing vulnerabilities. [2] For Snyk, activation is measured by aggregate level activity around fixes, which is a team-level activity. [2]

## Focusing on Existing User Retention

 The retention rate of existing users is a bigger lever for growth than new user acquisition. [3]
