# Activation Guidance for B2B Products
=====================================

As a B2B product manager, defining what it means for a user to be "activated" on your platform is crucial for determining the effectiveness of your onboarding process and long-term retention strategies.

## Establishing a Clear Activation Metric

Airtable's activation team focused on a single north star metric: what they called their team activation metric. This metric was determined to be highly correlated with long-term retention and required a number of things to be true, including substance built, persistence, and collaboration on a weekly cadence [1].

### Recommendation:
Identify a specific, measurable milestone that indicates a user is engaged with your platform.

## Defining Activation Milestones

Ben Williams, VP of Product at Snyk, defines activation as indicative of the team forming a habit around the usage of Snyk, specifically fixing vulnerabilities [2]. This metric is important because security is a team sport, and teams may have different members fulfilling different parts of the activation journey.

### Recommendation:
Focus on defining activation milestones that indicate a user is engaged with your platform, and consider the team's usage and retention metrics.

## Prioritizing Existing User Retention

Crystal Widjaja, former Growth Lead at Gojek, emphasizes the importance of existing user retention, particularly for products with daily frequency [3]. This is because retention compounds and builds daily habits, making it a critical lever for growth.

### Recommendation:
Prioritize existing user retention, especially for products with daily frequency, and focus on activation milestones that indicate a user is engaged with your platform.

## Step Out of Yourself and Recognize User Motivations

 By giving users a chance to succeed, you may find that it works out really well.

### Recommendation:
Step out of yourself and recognize user motivations and stages of the journey. Give users a chance to succeed, and focus on activation milestones that indicate a user is engaged with your platform.

## Designing for Onboarding

 Think about the first introduction, how you will discover the product, and how you will frame the experience.

### Recommendation:
Design for onboarding, considering how you introduce something and frame the experience. Think about the first introduction, how you will discover the product, and how you will frame the experience.
