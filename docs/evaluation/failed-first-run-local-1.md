**Unlocking User Activation in B2B Product Management: A Data-Driven Approach**

As a B2B product manager, you know that a significant challenge lies in driving user activation and retention in a competitive market. With the rise of alternative solutions and ever-evolving customer needs, it's easy to get stuck in a rut of feature-obsessed thinking. However, the data suggests that a more nuanced approach is necessary to unlock user engagement and drive growth. In this essay, we'll explore the key factors that influence user activation, provide actionable strategies, and shed light on successful examples in the B2B space.

**The Importance of Focusing on Marginal Users**

Research suggests that optimal user retention rates for B2B products lie between 20% and 30% [1]. This may seem surprisingly low, but it's essential to understand that marginal users – those who are not yet fully engaged – hold the key to scaling growth. Anuj Rathi, full-stack PM at Swiggy, emphasizes the importance of focusing on these users when optimizing onboarding: "Product managers must understand category consumers who are not yet in the market or buying your products, and be able to craft a compelling positioning statement to attract those users" [2].

To put this into practice, consider identifying your marginal users and analyzing their behavior, pain points, and motivations. Develop a deep understanding of their needs and craft a clear value proposition that resonates with them. This will help you create a more effective onboarding process that gently guides users toward adoption.

**The Power of Category Focus**

Anuj Rathi's emphasis on category focus highlights the importance of understanding the broader market landscape. When you focus on what your product can do for the next segment of users, you create a more compelling positioning statement that sets you apart from competing solutions. For instance, Airtable, a collaboration tool with seat-based pricing, uses a workspace activation metric to measure user engagement and growth [3].

To implement this strategy, identify your target audience and analyze their needs, pain points, and motivations. Develop a clear value proposition that speaks to these users and create a positioning statement that resonates with them. This will help you differentiate your product in a crowded market and drive user engagement.

**Onboarding Strategies for Success**

Lauryn Isford, Head of Growth at Airtable, emphasizes the importance of choosing an activation metric that aligns with your product's growth goals. For collaboration tools like Airtable, workspace activation metrics like the number of boards created or Airtables used can be more effective than user-specific metrics.

When developing your onboarding strategy, consider the following practical steps:

*   **Analyze user behavior**: Study your users' behavior, pain points, and motivations to create a more effective onboarding experience.
*   **Choose the right activation metric**: Select an activation metric that aligns with your product's growth goals and user behavior.
*   **Develop a clear value proposition**: Craft a clear value proposition that resonates with your target audience and sets your product apart from competing solutions.
*   **Focus on marginal users**: Identify and analyze your marginal users to create a more effective onboarding process that guides them toward adoption.
*   **Test and iterate**: Continuously test and iterate your onboarding strategy to ensure it's meeting your growth goals and providing a better user experience.

**Conclusion**

Unlocking user activation in B2B product management requires a nuanced approach that focuses on marginal users, category focus, and effective onboarding strategies. By analyzing user behavior, choosing the right activation metric, developing a clear value proposition, and focusing on marginal users, you can create a more effective onboarding experience that drives growth and engagement. As a B2B product manager, it's essential to stay data-driven, adapt to changing market conditions, and continually iterate on your onboarding strategy to ensure it remains effective.
