# Activation Milestones for Improved User Engagement

## Defining Activation Milestones

Defining a clear activation milestone is crucial for improving user engagement. According to Lauryn Isford, Head of Growth at Airtable, every growth team needs a north star metric to focus on what the business results are that they are working to drive in their team. [1]

### Step 1: Establish a Clear Activation Metric

Identify a metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention and holds a relatively high bar, requiring substance and persistence from users. At Airtable, the team used a representative team of users being activated on the product and using it in a way that suggested long-term retention as their activation milestone. [1]

### Step 2: Focus on Team Activation

Consider focusing on team activation, as security is a team sport. This approach helps to create a habit around the usage of the product, rather than just logging in. [2]

### Step 3: Define Activation Milestones for Your Product

Define activation milestones for your product based on your specific use case. For example, at Snyk, the activation milestone is when a user fixes vulnerabilities, rather than just finding them. [2]

### Step 4: Consider the Onboarding Experience

The onboarding experience can be a key lever for retention and activation. Consider designing the onboarding experience from the user's perspective, asking questions like "What will it be like for someone to come in here?" and "What will I be asking them to integrate with?" [3]

### Step 5: Focus on Existing User Retention

For products with daily frequency, focus most of your energy on existing user retention mechanics. This is because retention compounds and builds daily habits. [4]

### Step 6: Use a PLG Motion

Consider using a product-led growth (PLG) motion, such as a free version, free trial, or open-source product, to create a vehicle for activation. [5]

### Practical Recommendations

* Establish a clear activation metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention.
* Focus on team activation, as security is a team sport.
* Define activation milestones for your product based on your specific use case.
* Consider the onboarding experience and design it from the user's perspective.
* Focus on existing user retention mechanics for products with daily frequency.
* Use a PLG motion to create a vehicle for activation.

## Takeaway

Defining a clear activation milestone is crucial for improving user engagement. By following these practical recommendations, you can create a successful activation strategy for your product.
