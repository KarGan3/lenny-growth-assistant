**Unlocking User Activation: Key Takeaways for Your Team**

activation: The process of creating a new user or user group and bringing them into the product's ecosystem.

*   **Set the right context**: Make it clear that even though results are important, it's a team sport.  Encourage team members to work together and share knowledge.
*   **Lead by example**: Demonstrate a "team-first" approach by taking on additional responsibilities and sharing your expertise with others.
*   **Be open-minded**: Don't be afraid to adjust your approach as new data emerges.  Try different metrics and signals to measure success.

activation milestones: A key indicator of a user's level of engagement with your product.

*   **Focus on building habits**:  It's not just about logging in or finding vulnerabilities.  It's about deriving core value and fixing vulnerabilities.
*   **Work as a team**:  Security is often a team effort.  Expect different team members to fulfill different parts of the activation journey.
*   **Adjust your approach as needed**:  Aggregate-level activity around fixing vulnerabilities can be a good metric to measure.
