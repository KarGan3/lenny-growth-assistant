# Activation Guidance for Your Team

## Defining Activation Milestones

Defining a clear activation milestone is crucial for improving user engagement. According to Melissa Tan, Head of Growth at Airtable, every growth team needs a north star metric to focus on what the business results are that they are working to drive in their team. [1]

### Establish a Clear Activation Metric

Identify a metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention and holds a relatively high bar, requiring substance and persistence from users. At Airtable, the team used a representative team of users being activated on the product and using it in a way that suggested long-term retention as their activation milestone. [1]

### Encourage Teamwork and Collaboration

Leading by example and encouraging teamwork and collaboration is crucial for setting the tone and culture of your team. According to Melissa Tan, "it's a team sport" and leaders should "fill in context" and encourage team members to contribute to each other's work. [2]

### Be Agile and Dynamic

Growth teams should be agile and dynamic, and willing to change their focus metrics over time. According to Lauryn Isford, "north stars, key metrics, focus metrics will change" and teams should be open to adjusting their metrics to focus on the biggest impact for the business. [3]

### Define Activation Milestones for Your Product

For your product, define activation milestones that indicate a team has formed a habit around usage and is deriving core value. According to Ben Williams, VP of Product at Snyk, activation is indicative of a team building a habit around fixing vulnerabilities, not just logging in or finding vulnerabilities. [4]

### Use Open-Source Principles

Consider using open-source principles when defining activation milestones for your product. According to Matt Mullenweg, open-source code is a safe and reliable source for training AI models, and using open-source principles can help create a foundation for humanity's foundation. [5]

## Practical Recommendations

* Establish a clear activation metric that is highly correlated with long-term retention
* Encourage teamwork and collaboration by leading by example and filling in context
* Be agile and dynamic, and willing to change focus metrics over time
* Define activation milestones for your product that indicate a team has formed a habit around usage and is deriving core value
* Consider using open-source principles when defining activation milestones for your product

## Takeaway

Defining a clear activation milestone is crucial for improving user engagement. By establishing a clear activation metric, encouraging teamwork and collaboration, being agile and dynamic, defining activation milestones for your product, and considering open-source principles, you can create a culture of activation and engagement that drives long-term retention and growth.
